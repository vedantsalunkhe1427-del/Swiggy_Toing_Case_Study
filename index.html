⚠ The Opening Dilemma

Online food delivery in metros stopped growing because extra fees doubled the bill.

Between ₹14 platform fees, ₹25 rain charges, packaging surcharges, and delivery fees, an everyday ₹99 lunch exploded to ₹193 at checkout. Toing is Swiggy's radical answer: a standalone app stripping out extras. The unanswered question is logistics economics.

03 Diagnosis · Root Cause Analysis
Funnel Leaks · Structural Diagnosis

Why did core food delivery
hit a growth ceiling?

In Q1 FY27, Swiggy food delivery GOV growth slightly missed consensus estimates and trailed Zomato. Before building sub-brands, every PM must answer: was stagnation caused by market saturation, fee fatigue, or an unsolvable logistics cost floor?

Where the food delivery funnel actually leaks Funnel Diagnosis
1. Hunger Intent
Vast (Daily)
Hundreds of millions of Indians eat lunch outside the home daily. No shortage of aggregate demand.
→
2. App Browse
High Penetration
Users open Swiggy or Zomato to browse local restaurants, ratings, and menu items.
→
3. Cart Checkout
Severe Leak
Meal ₹99 + Platform ₹14.99 + Packaging ₹19 + Delivery ₹35 = ₹168+. User abandons cart in disgust.
→
4. Habit Formation
Frequency Ceiling
Ordering stays an occasional weekend splurge (1-2x/month) rather than daily lunch habit.
The Diagnosis: High platform fees created an insurmountable psychological barrier. You cannot market or discount your way past a 55% checkout surcharge on daily meals.
Three Strategic Hypotheses Validation
Is it an advertising problem?
✕ Rejected
Both Swiggy and Zomato have near 100% brand recall in tier-1/2 cities. More brand spend only acquired users who churned after the first order.
Is it a restaurant selection issue?
~ Partly
Local dhabas and pocket-friendly darshinis were absent because 25% commissions wiped out their razor-thin margins.
Is it fee compounding?
✓ Confirmed Root
When extras equal 50–90% of the food price, value perception collapses. Toing strips extras to attack this exact root cause.
04 Solution Design · Standalone Architecture
Product Mechanics · Realistic Device Mockup

Why a standalone app
beats an in-app budget tab.

Swiggy deliberately chose an independent app over a tab inside the flagship app. A budget tab would have triggered immediate cannibalization, causing 20 million affluent users to demand zero platform fees and eroding ₹202 Cr in quarterly food delivery profits.

✕ The Swiggy Main App Dilemma

Compounding fee stack

  1. Customer selects ₹99 veg thali
  2. Restaurant packaging charge added (+₹19)
  3. Mandatory platform fee applied (+₹14.99)
  4. Delivery partner fee (+₹35)
  5. Surge/Rain fee applied during peak (+₹25)
  6. Checkout hits ₹193 — user uninstalls or walks to the dhaba
High price friction · 55% markup over kitchen price
✓ The Toing Standalone Architecture

Strips out junk fees entirely

  1. Curated catalog strictly under ₹99
  2. Zero packaging charge negotiated upfront
  3. Flat ₹12 platform fee; free delivery over ₹99
  4. No surge pricing or rain surcharges
  5. Checkout stays locked at ₹124
  6. Riders cross-routed from idle Instamart/Bolt hours

The Four Operational Pillars

1 Interleaved Fleet Routing

Routing riders between morning grocery peaks on Instamart, afternoon budget lunches on Toing, and evening quick deliveries on Bolt.

2 Zero Packaging Guarantee

Standardized bio-degradable meal box bulk procurement provided directly by Swiggy, removing merchant markups.

3 Hyper-Local Density Batching

Clustering delivery orders in student hubs (Symbiosis, Viman Nagar) so one rider delivers 2-3 orders per trip.

4 Merchant Sponsored Ads

Monetizing top-of-feed placement to replace high commission take-rates with voluntary advertising auctions.

08 Go-To-Market · Phased Roadmap
Operational Delivery · GTM

A discipline-first phased rollout.

Replicating Instamart's turnaround journey (from -4.6% to break-even in May 2026). Each phase is governed by clear financial gates:

Stage 1 · Months 0–6 · Proven Launch

Standalone Budget Foundation (Completed)

Launched Pune pilot with 500 initial downloads; expanded to 1,000+ restaurants with meals under ₹99, zero packaging fees, and flat ₹12 platform fee. Rapidly scaled to 50 cities with 2/3 of users being first-time online food delivery consumers.

📊 Gate Passed: Reached 50 cities & 66% first-timer ratio.
Stage 2 · Months 6–12 · Logistics Density

Fleet Interleaving & Order Batching

Dynamically cross-route riders during afternoon low-order periods between morning grocery peaks on Instamart and evening quick-bites on Bolt. Cluster orders in university campuses and IT corridors to achieve 1.8x batching efficiency.

📊 Gate Threshold: Achieve >1.8 orders/drop and drop cost below ₹22.
Stage 3 · Months 12–18 · Monetization & Defense

Merchant Ads & Bengaluru Fortification

Roll out sponsored merchant ad auctions to generate high-margin platform income. Lock in long-term exclusivity with top budget eateries in Bengaluru to block Rapido Ownly from expanding beyond its 10% market share.

📊 Gate Threshold: City cohort unit contribution margin >0% across 80% of live markets.
09 Strategic Moat · The Ultimate Prize
Strategic Value · The Endgame

Three prizes, one standalone app.

Toing is not just a defensive barrier against Rapido Ownly; it is an aggressive top-of-funnel customer acquisition weapon for the entire Swiggy ecosystem.

Defensive Flank Protection

🔒 The Moat

Shared Infrastructure Density

A startup cannot easily duplicate a 400,000+ rider mesh and 1,171 Instamart dark stores. By interleaving riders between morning groceries, noon budget lunches, and evening quick-bites, Swiggy drives down fixed cost per hour lower than any standalone food rival can match.

"Affordability is the single largest unlock for the category in India. Swiggy's challenge is not proving demand for a ₹99 meal—it is mastering the logistics math that lets it survive."
